# AWS cost: now vs after (estimate at us-east-1 list prices, not a bill)

Nothing here is applied. `terraform plan` must be run by the owner's Mac
(`launch.tfvars`); `terraform` is not installed in the cloud session, so the
new `api_spot` / `worker_spot` wiring is reviewed by eye, not validated.

| Item | Now, quiet (1 api, 1 worker) | After, quiet | At 10 api + 4 worker tasks, now | At 10 + 4, after (Spot above the first) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| API tasks (ARM 0.5 vCPU / 1 GB ≈ $14.4) | $14 | $14 | $144 | $14 + 9 × ≈$4.5 = $55 |
| Worker (ARM 0.5 vCPU / 4 GB ≈ $22) | $22 | $22 | $88 | $22 + 3 × ≈$7 = $43 |
| Admin, ALB, NAT, WAF, Redis, RDS, TURN, logs | ≈ $110–130 | same | same | same |
| Total | ≈ $150–170 | ≈ $150–170 | ≈ $340 | ≈ $210 |

Honest reading: Spot does not lower the quiet-hour bill (the first task of
each service stays on regular Fargate so the app is never down). It cuts the
cost of growth by about 60 %. Spot tasks can be stopped with a 2-minute
warning: the ALB drains them, the queue retries the job.

Already in place and kept: ARM64, CPU 60 % and requests-per-task autoscaling
(out in 60 s, in after 10 min), min 1 task, hard cap, monthly budget guard
at $200.

Not changed, with the reason:
- NAT (~$33): needed for private tasks; public subnets would save it but weaken isolation.
- ALB (~$20) and WAF (~$10–15): the entry point and the only abuse shield; no cheaper managed equivalent.
- Night scale-down: useless at min 1 (a service cannot go below 1 without downtime); it matters only after min_tasks ≥ 2.
- CloudFront in front of the API: only public GETs (config, i18n, directories) can be cached there and they are already small; per-user reads (feed, chats) cannot. Revisit when public traffic dominates.
