# QR login (sign in on the website from the phone)

## Mobile (apps/mobile) — owner 2026-10-22

What the phone does:

- **Link shape.** The website's QR code encodes `https://lawbid.dev/qr/<nonce>`.
  The phone understands it in three places:
  - the in-app scanner (Profile → QR → «Сканировать», `features/qr`):
    `classifyQr` → `WebLoginQrTarget` → the approval screen;
  - the phone's own camera app (universal link / Android App Link):
    `core/deeplinks/deep_link.dart` parses `/qr/<nonce>` into
    `WebLoginDeepLink`, `DeepLinkController` opens `/qr/<nonce>`;
  - a browser on the phone (no app): the same route renders the public
    «open the app» page, no account needed.
- **Older payload.** `https://lawbid.dev/q/<id>` (the first QR format) is
  still accepted everywhere; both land on `WebLoginLinkScreen`, which calls
  `GET /auth/qr/{id}` for the preview and `POST /auth/qr/{id}/approve` /
  `/reject`. The id in the path is the nonce.
- **Routes.** go_router: `/q/:id` and `/qr/:nonce` (`DeepLinkRoutes`).
  `/qr/my` and `/qr/scan` stay the app's own screens; a nonce is at least
  16 characters, so they never collide. The router guard lets a signed-out
  phone see the page (`AppRoutes.isWebLoginLink`).
- **Platform files.** Android: `<data android:pathPrefix="/qr/" />` in
  `AndroidManifest.xml` (auto-verified against assetlinks.json). iOS:
  `/qr/*` in the Associated Domains file; both files are generated by
  `scripts/seo/well-known.mjs` and the checked-in copies in `docs/deeplinks`
  must equal the generated ones (tests in
  `apps/mobile/test/core/deeplinks/deep_link_platform_config_test.dart`
  and `scripts/seo/test/well-known.test.mjs`).
- **Signed out.** A phone without an account drops the link (the code lives
  two minutes); the page then only says «open the app and sign in».

Owner steps (plain words): nothing new for iOS — the domain file is
published by the «SEO pages» workflow. For Android the repository variable
`ANDROID_APP_SHA256` must hold the Play signing certificate's SHA-256
(docs/deeplinks/README.md §2); until then Android opens the link in the
browser, and the browser page sends the person to the app.
