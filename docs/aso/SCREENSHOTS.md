# Screenshots and preview video — plan

No code change: the owner (or a helper on the Mac) captures them from the real app in the demo world (`docs/ops/demo-world.md`). Rules: invented but plausible sample names, no real client data, no real attorney photos without their written consent, no fake reviews or star counts shown as proof, no "best / top / #1 / guaranteed / free consultation". The first frame states what the app is, not what it promises.

## Sizes (check the current numbers in the console before exporting)

- App Store: the 6.9-inch iPhone set (1320 x 2868) is the one App Store Connect asks for; smaller iPhone sizes are scaled from it. 3-10 screenshots, first three matter most in search. iPad only if the app supports iPad.
- Google Play: phone screenshots 2-8 (use 8), JPEG or 24-bit PNG, feature graphic 1024 x 500, icon 512 x 512. Tablet screenshots only if you want the tablet listing.
- Captions are drawn in the image, large type, sentence case, max 6 words, English (US).

## Order and captions (same eight frames for both stores)

| # | Capture (screen, state) | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Post-a-case form filled with a sample matter (tenant repair) | Describe your legal matter |
| 2 | Offers list on that case, three sample attorneys with price and approach | Compare offers side by side |
| 3 | Attorney profile: licensed states, practice areas, Verified badge | Check licensed states and practice areas |
| 4 | Chat with an attorney (text, document, voice note) | Message attorneys in the app |
| 5 | The Verified badge sheet ("license checked against the state registry") | What Verified means |
| 6 | Attorney post / short video in the feed | Attorneys share posts and videos |
| 7 | Attorney side: new cases in my states, alerts | For attorneys: new cases in your state |
| 8 | Plain frame with the disclaimer text and logo | LawBid is not a law firm |

Do not show: ratings as proof of quality, "free" badges next to an attorney, any phone number or e-mail, any real person's case.
