LawBid is an online marketplace where people describe a legal matter and attorneys licensed in their state respond with offers. You compare the offers and choose whom to talk to.

FOR PEOPLE WITH A LEGAL MATTER
• Post your case in a few minutes: what happened, where, and what you need. Your contact details stay hidden until you choose to share them.
• Attorneys licensed in your state send offers with their approach and price.
• Read attorney profiles: licensed states, practice areas, languages and reviews from clients.
• Message attorneys, share documents and voice notes, or call in the app.
• Posting a case and receiving offers is free for clients.

PRACTICE AREAS
Family and divorce, child custody, immigration, criminal defense, DUI, personal injury, landlord and tenant, housing and eviction, wills and estates, bankruptcy and debt, employment, business and contracts, consumer protection, insurance claims, and more than 40 areas in total.

COVERAGE
United States, all 50 states and Washington, D.C. You see attorneys licensed in the state of your matter.

FOR ATTORNEYS
• See new cases in your states and practice areas, with instant alerts.
• Send offers and keep each conversation in one place.
• Share posts and short videos on your profile.
• Flat subscription: no per-lead fees and no commission on your fees.

VERIFIED ATTORNEYS
A Verified badge means the attorney's license was checked against the state registry. It is not a rating and not a promise of any result.

LawBid is an online marketplace, not a law firm. It does not give legal advice and does not guarantee any result. An attorney-client relationship begins only when you hire an attorney.
