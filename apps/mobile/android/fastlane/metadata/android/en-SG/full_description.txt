LawBid helps you find a lawyer in the United States. Describe your legal issue for free, get offers from licensed, verified attorneys in your state, compare them and hire the one you trust.

HOW IT WORKS FOR CLIENTS
• Post your case in a few minutes: what happened, where, and what you need. Your contact details stay hidden.
• Attorneys licensed in your state send you offers with their price and approach.
• Compare profiles, reviews, experience and practice areas.
• Chat, share documents and voice notes, or call the attorney right in the app.
• Posting a case and receiving offers is free for clients.

PRACTICE AREAS
Divorce and child custody, child support, adoption, immigration (green card, visas, citizenship, asylum, deportation defense), criminal defense, DUI, traffic tickets, personal injury and car accidents, medical malpractice, workplace injuries, wrongful termination, eviction and housing, real estate, wills, trusts and inheritance, bankruptcy and debt, taxes and the IRS, business and contracts, trademarks and patents, consumer protection, insurance claims, disability and veterans benefits, civil rights, lawsuits and appeals: more than 40 areas in total.

WHERE IT WORKS
LawBid works across the United States, in all 50 states and Washington, D.C., including California, New York, Florida, Texas, Illinois, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Washington, Georgia and Arizona. You only see attorneys licensed where your case is.

FOR ATTORNEYS
• New cases from your state and practice areas, with instant alerts.
• Send offers, negotiate and keep every conversation in one place.
• Grow your audience with posts and short videos on your profile.
• Flat subscription: no per-lead fees and no commission on your fees.

TRUST AND SAFETY
• Every attorney's license is checked before they can send offers.
• Verified badges for attorneys and clients.
• Contact details stay protected until you choose to share them.
• Block, report and support are always one tap away.

The app interface is in English and Russian.

LawBid is an online marketplace, not a law firm. It does not provide legal advice and does not refer clients to attorneys. An attorney-client relationship begins only after you hire an attorney.
