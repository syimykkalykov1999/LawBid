I-LawBid ikusiza ukuthi uthole ummeli e-United States. Chaza inkinga yakho yezomthetho mahhala, uthole iminikelo evela kubameli abanelayisensi neqinisekisiwe esifundeni sakho, ubaqhathanise bese uqasha lowo omethembayo.

INDLELA ESEBENZA NGAYO KUMAKHASIMENDE
• Faka icala lakho ngemizuzu embalwa: kwenzekeni, kuphi nokuthi udingani. Imininingwane yakho yokuxhumana ihlala ifihliwe.
• Abameli abanelayisensi esifundeni sakho bakuthumelela iminikelo enentengo nendlela yabo yokusebenza.
• Qhathanisa amaphrofayela, izibuyekezo, ulwazi nemikhakha yokusebenza.
• Xoxa, thumela amadokhumenti nemilayezo yezwi, noma ushayele ummeli ngqo ku-app.
• Ukufaka icala nokuthola iminikelo kumahhala kumakhasimende.

IMIKHAKHA YOMTHETHO
Ukwehlukanisa umshado nokunakekelwa kwezingane, isondlo sezingane, ukutholwa kwezingane, ukufuduka (i-green card, ama-visa, ubuzwe, isiphephelo, ukuvikelwa ekudingisweni), ukuvikelwa emacaleni obugebengu, i-DUI, izinhlawulo zomgwaqo, ukulimala komuntu nezingozi zezimoto, amaphutha odokotela, ukulimala emsebenzini, ukuxoshwa emsebenzini ngokungafanele, ukuxoshwa endlini nezindaba zezindlu, izakhiwo nomhlaba, amawili, ama-trust nefa, ukuqothuka nezikweletu, intela ne-IRS, ibhizinisi nezinkontileka, izimpawu zokuhweba namalungelo obunikazi, ukuvikelwa kwabathengi, izimangalo zomshwalense, izibonelelo zokukhubazeka nezamasosha asebenzile, amalungelo omphakathi, amacala asenkantolo nokudlulisa amacala: imikhakha engaphezu kwengu-40 sekukonke.

LAPHO ISEBENZA KHONA
I-LawBid isebenza kulo lonke elase-United States, ezifundeni zonke ezingu-50 nase-Washington, D.C., kuhlanganise ne-California, New York, Florida, Texas, Illinois, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Washington, Georgia ne-Arizona. Ubona kuphela abameli abanelayisensi lapho kukhona khona icala lakho.

KUBAMELI
• Amacala amasha avela esifundeni sakho nasemikhakheni yakho, nezaziso ezisheshayo.
• Thumela iminikelo, xoxisana futhi ugcine yonke ingxoxo endaweni eyodwa.
• Khulisa izethameli zakho ngokuthumela okuthunyelwe namavidiyo amafushane kuphrofayela yakho.
• Okubhaliselwe kwentengo engaguquki: ayikho imali ngekhasimende ngalinye futhi ayikho ikhomishini emalini yakho.

UKWETHEMBA NOKUPHEPHA
• Ilayisensi yommeli ngamunye iyahlolwa ngaphambi kokuthi athumele iminikelo.
• Amabheji okuqinisekisa abameli namakhasimende.
• Imininingwane yokuxhumana ivikelekile kuze kube yilapho uzikhethela ukuyabelana.
• Ukuvimba, ukubika nosizo kuhlale kuyithinto elilodwa kude.
