# Cookie and SDK Notice

*DRAFT for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Version: draft-2026-10-25.*

This notice covers the LawBid website and apps.

## What we use
- **Strictly necessary**: session and security cookies, login tokens, load balancing, fraud prevention, your language and consent choices. These do not need consent.
- **App SDKs**: Firebase (push notifications and app integrity checks) and Sentry (crash and error reports). Crash reports can include device model, OS, app version and technical logs. We configure them to avoid collecting message content, screen recordings or keystrokes and to scrub personal data. [Confirm before publishing.]
- **Analytics and advertising**: we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies and do not sell or share data for advertising. [If product analytics are added to the website, they load only after you accept them.]

## Your choices
You can choose "Accept" or "Only necessary" in the cookie banner on the website, change it at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer, or block cookies in your browser (some features may stop working). We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out request. We do not respond to Do Not Track because we do not track you across sites.

## How long
Session cookies end when you close the browser. Consent choices are kept up to 12 months. Login tokens last up to [30] days.
