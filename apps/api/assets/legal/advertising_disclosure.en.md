# Advertising Disclosure

*DRAFT for review by a licensed New York attorney with legal-ethics experience. Not legal advice. Version: draft-2026-10-25.*

**LawBid does not recommend, endorse or vouch for any attorney.** LawBid is not a lawyer referral service. Attorneys on LawBid pay LawBid flat fees for access to the platform and for advertising. The fees do not depend on whether you hire the attorney or on what you pay the attorney, and LawBid takes no part of any legal fee.

## What you see and why
- **License checked**: we compare the license an attorney reports with the public record of the issuing state and show the date and source. It is a record check only; it is not an assessment of skill or a recommendation.
- **Order of results**: results are ordered by the following disclosed factors applied by the same rules to everyone: [relevance to your search terms, practice area and location, whether the attorney is available, response time, and recency]. We do not rank by our opinion of an attorney's quality.
- **Paid placements**: attorneys can pay for a boost that shows their profile or post more often or higher. Boosted items are labeled **"Sponsored"** and are **attorney advertising**. A boost does not mean we vouch for the attorney.
- **Bids**: attorneys buy bid credits (a flat price per response) to respond to matters clients post. A credit pays for the chance to respond, not for a result.
- **Reviews**: written by clients, moderated only on neutral grounds (fake, paid, unlawful, private data). Attorneys may reply. We do not remove reviews because they are negative.
- **Best answer in public Q&A**: chosen by the person who asked. It is that person's opinion only.
- **Badges** (for example years licensed, number of reviews, response time) are based on objective data shown with the date. We do not give awards like "top" or "best".
- **Attorney advertising**: content created by attorneys (profiles, posts, videos, bids, answers) is attorney advertising. [In New York: Attorney Advertising. Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.]
- **Partners and referrers**: people who promote LawBid for rewards must disclose the relationship (FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255). Lawyers who are partners may not receive rewards tied to clients retained or to legal fees.

You should check an attorney's license, ask about fees, and read the engagement letter before you hire.
