# Community Guidelines

*DRAFT for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Version: draft-2026-10-25.*

LawBid is a place for people to find legal help. Keep it safe and honest.

## Not allowed
- Threats, harassment, hate, stalking, doxxing.
- **Sexual content involving minors.** We report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and law enforcement.
- **Intimate images of a person shared without their consent**, including AI-generated ones. See "Report an intimate image" below.
- Violence, self-harm encouragement, terrorism, illegal goods.
- Impersonating a lawyer, a client, LawBid or anyone else. Claiming to be licensed when you are not.
- Fake, paid or incentivized reviews, fake followers or likes, review swaps, threats to make someone remove a review.
- Spam, scams, phishing, asking for payment outside the Service to avoid its rules, scraping.
- Sharing other people's private data or confidential information without permission.
- Content that infringes copyright or trademark. See the DMCA Policy.
- Recording or sharing a private call or chat without consent where the law requires it.
- Practicing law without a license, or giving individualized legal advice where you are not licensed.

## For attorneys
Your profile, posts, answers and bids are advertising. Follow your professional rules. No guarantees of results. Do not contact people who did not ask for contact. Do not reveal client confidences when replying to reviews.

## Reviews
Honest reviews from real experience are welcome, positive or negative. We remove reviews only for neutral reasons: fake or paid, unlawful, private data, off-topic. We do not remove a review because it is negative.

## Reporting
Use Report on any case, profile, post, reel, story, review, message or answer, or email abuse@lawbid.dev. We review reports, and may remove content, warn, limit features, suspend or ban accounts, and report to bars, payment providers or police. You can appeal a decision from the notice we send.

## Report an intimate image shared without consent (Take It Down)
If an intimate image of you was posted without your consent, choose Report, "Intimate image without consent", or email takedown@lawbid.dev from any address with: a statement that you or the person you represent is the person in the image and did not consent, where the image is located (link or screenshot), a way to contact you, and your signature (typing your name is enough). **We will remove the image within 48 hours** and make reasonable efforts to remove identical copies. A bad-faith request may be refused.

## Child safety
If you see a child in danger, call 911. Report child sexual abuse material in the app or to abuse@lawbid.dev.

## Repeat violations
Accounts with repeated or serious violations are closed.
