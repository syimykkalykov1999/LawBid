# Client Terms

*DRAFT for review by a licensed attorney. Not legal advice. Version: draft-2026-10-25.*

These Terms apply to people who use LawBid to find an attorney or ask legal questions. They add to the Terms of Service.

1. **LawBid is not a law firm.** We do not give legal advice and do not recommend any attorney. Attorneys on LawBid are independent. We show whether an attorney's license was checked against the state's public record; that is not a judgment of quality.
2. **No attorney-client relationship until you agree.** Posting a matter, asking a question, chatting with or receiving a bid from an attorney does not create an attorney-client relationship. It starts only when an attorney agrees to represent you, normally in a written engagement letter. Until then, the attorney may not know of conflicts and may decline.
3. **Be careful what you post.** Do not post confidential details in public places (public questions, public profile, posts). A case description may be visible to many attorneys. Share sensitive details only after you choose an attorney.
4. **You choose.** You may hire any attorney, on or off LawBid. LawBid takes no part of the legal fee. You decide what to pay and the terms in the engagement letter. Ask about fees, how advance payments are held, and refund rules.
5. **Public answers are general information.** Answers in public Q&A are not advice for your situation. "Best answer" is picked by the person who asked, not by LawBid.
6. **Reviews.** Reviews must be honest and based on your own experience. Do not accept payment or gifts for a review. You can review honestly without fear of penalty. We remove reviews only on neutral grounds (fake, paid, unlawful, private data).
7. **Calls and recording.** We do not record calls. Do not record an attorney or anyone else without everyone's consent. Some states require everyone's consent.
8. **Translation.** Translations are automatic and may be wrong. Ask your attorney to confirm important points in the original language.
9. **Payments.** If you pay an attorney through LawBid, Stripe processes the payment. Disputes about fees or work are between you and the attorney; contact us and we will share payment records. Stars and other digital items have no cash value.
10. **Subscriptions.** Client subscriptions renew automatically as shown at checkout until you cancel in the app.
11. **Safety.** Do not share passwords, card numbers or one-time codes in chat. Report anything suspicious. In an emergency call 911. LawBid is not an emergency service. For court deadlines, act quickly: missing a deadline may cost you your rights.
12. **Your information.** What we collect and who sees it is explained in the Privacy Policy. Staff do not read private chats routinely.
