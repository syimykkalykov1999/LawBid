## 3. Professional rules are your responsibility

You must comply with the rules of professional conduct in every jurisdiction where you practice, including:
- **Advertising and communications** (for example New York Rules 7.1 to 7.5): your profile, posts, videos, bids, answers and boosts are your advertising. You must add any label (such as "Attorney Advertising"), disclaimer (such as "prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome"), office address and retention of copies that your rules require. No guarantees of results, no misleading comparisons, no unearned "specialist" or "expert" claims.
- **Solicitation**: contact only people who posted a matter, asked a question, replied to you, or otherwise asked to hear from you. No unsolicited messages to people who did not.
- **Fees**: you may not agree to pay anyone a share of your legal fee or pay for a recommendation of you. LawBid takes no share of your fees. Your subscription, bid-pack and boost charges are flat, do not depend on whether you are hired or on what you earn, and are payment for platform access and advertising. Written engagement letters and fee rules are your responsibility.
- **Client funds**: advance fees and funds held for clients go into the trust account (IOLTA) rules that apply to you. LawBid does not hold client funds.
- **Conflicts and prospective clients**: a person who posts a matter may reveal facts before you run a conflict check. You must protect that information and decline when you have a conflict.
- **Supervision of assistants**: you are responsible for your assistants and for what is sent in your name. Assistants must not give legal advice.
- **Unauthorized practice**: do not give legal advice in states where you are not admitted.

## 4. Confidentiality, messages and AI features

- You are responsible for protecting your clients' confidences. The Service is a communication platform, not a vault. Do not post confidential information in public places.

- Machine translation and other automated features send message text to third-party providers. Do not use them for a conversation where you or your client have not accepted that. You may turn them off per conversation.
- Do not enter client confidences into any AI feature unless your own rules and your client's informed consent allow it (see ABA Formal Opinion 512).

## 5. Reviews and answers

- You may not describe LawBid's license check as an endorsement.
